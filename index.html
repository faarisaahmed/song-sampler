How it works

Songs. Search results are human-made karaoke MIDIs from the Lyrics MIDI Dataset, each pulled out of the dataset's zip on Hugging Face with a single range request. The melody track is the one that lines up with the lyrics, or, for files without lyrics, the one a small neural network picks (only songs it's at least 70% sure about are listed).

Background music. The real recording comes from iTunes (a 30-second preview, or your own file for the whole song). An AI model (UVR MDX-Net, 67 MB, downloaded once) removes the vocals on your device. The MIDI is then lined up with the recording with dynamic time warping, comparing both the harmony and the sung melody, and moved to the recording's key.

Syllables. The melody is cut into passages at rests. Every passage starts with o i and ends with a, and the middle follows how the original meme flows (oiiaioiiiai), with long notes leaning toward a drawn-out "aaa".

Pitch. Each note is made from the original cat clip with PSOLA pitch shifting, so pitch changes without speeding the sound up or turning it into a chipmunk.

The OIIA sound comes from the spinning-cat meme. Songs: Lyrics MIDI Dataset by asigalov61 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Recordings: iTunes Search API. Vocal removal: UVR-MDX-NET Voc_FT from Ultimate Vocal Remover, run with onnxruntime-web.
